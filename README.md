#  Bioinformatics Sandbox

> A personal GitHub profile for projects that are *at least vaguely related* to bioinformatics.

---

##  What lives here

You’ll find repositories that may involve:

* **Bioinformatics & Computational Biology**
  Sequence analysis, genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics-adjacent work, or biological data wrangling.

* **Data Analysis & Statistics**
  Exploratory analyses, benchmarking scripts, visualizations, and statistical experiments inspired by biological problems.

* **Tooling & Automation**
  Small scripts, pipelines, and helpers for working with biological data (often opinionated, sometimes messy).

* **Learning Projects**
  Reimplementations, tutorials, paper-following code, and notebooks created to understand methods rather than to be production-ready.


##  Common tools & ecosystems

Depending on the project, you may see:

* **Languages**: Python
* **Libraries**: pandas, scikit-learn, Biopython
* **Formats**: FASTA/FASTQ, CSV/TSV
* **Environments**: Conda / Mamba, virtualenv

##  Repository status legend (informal)

* ✅ **Stable-ish** — usable with minimal friction
* 🧩 **Experimental** — APIs and ideas may change
* 🧪 **Prototype** — proof-of-concept or paper recreation
* 💤 **Archived** — kept for reference, not maintenance

(If no status is indicated, assume 🧪.)
